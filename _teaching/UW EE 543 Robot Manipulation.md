Co-instructor and teaching assistant for EE 543 Models of Robot Manipulation. This graduate course covers analytical and computational methods for robot arm manipulation. It asks how joint angles and velocities set the motion of the end effector and how to drive the joint motors to place and orient the gripper for a task such as picking.

Course Content
======
Coordinate transformations and rigid body motion. Forward and inverse kinematics. Differential kinematics and manipulator dynamics. Trajectory generation and motion planning. Teleoperation.
