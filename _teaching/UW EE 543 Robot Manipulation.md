Co-instructor and teaching assistant for EE 543 Models of Robot Manipulation in Winter 2026. The course covers analytical and computational methods for robot arm manipulation. It asks how joint motion sets the motion of the gripper and how to drive the joints to place the gripper for a task.

Course Content
======
- Coordinate transformations and rigid body motion
- Forward and inverse kinematics
- Differential kinematics and manipulator dynamics
- Trajectory generation and motion planning
- Teleoperation

Responsibilities
======
- Helped lead in-class practice problem sessions
- Ran exam review sessions
- Held lab sessions to help students with their robotic arm projects
