Sole-author review covering why prosthetic decoders degrade outside the lab (neural plasticity, motor learning, muscle fatigue, electrode displacement, cross-session drift, anatomical variability) and how closed-loop decoder adaptation, adaptive filtering, ensemble modeling, reinforcement learning, and transfer learning each address part of the problem. It argues that co-adaptive systems, where decoder updates interact with ongoing neural and motor plasticity, are needed to close the translational gap.
